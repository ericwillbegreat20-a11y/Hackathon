Campusline Hate UX Demo

Extract all files, then open index.html in Chrome or Edge. Keep every file in the same folder.
Assignment task: open Assignments to start a 60-second countdown. Enter an answer or choose a sample file, check exactly one submission route and the acknowledgment, then complete all four inline confirmations and the 10-second queue. Multiple routes can be checked while editing, but manual submission rejects them until only one remains.

At 60 seconds, the current draft is automatically submitted, even if empty or unacknowledged. The last checked route is kept; no selection defaults to Course portal. Closing/reopening the dialog does not reset the timer. A submitted assignment cannot be edited. Refresh resets the demo.

Payment demo: click Simulate payment to open MISSPAY.html. Keep that file beside index.html; it is a fictional simulation and does not process real payments.


Real camera demo: on MISSPAY Face Verification, click Start camera & detect face and allow browser camera access. Only local face presence is checked; no identity matching. Two consecutive positive frames complete verification and immediately close the camera. No photos, recordings, face features, or camera images are stored or uploaded. Stop/cancel, switching away, and scan timeout also close the camera. Camera access requires a secure context (localhost or HTTPS if your browser blocks local files). Keep pico.js, face-model.js, and face-camera.js beside MISSPAY.html.


Grades demo: open Grades to see 18 sample scores ranging from 50 to 90, sorted lowest first. Data Structures starts as the weakest subject (average 60.0). Its rows and a reminder to keep improving are highlighted. Course filters retain ascending order; new instructor grades join the same ordering and update the weakest-subject average. Ungraded assignments appear at the bottom.
