# CAMPUSLINE

> 
> Reverse Product Innovation | Track 02

**Headline**: A University Learning Platform That Frustrates You, Yet You Still Have to Use.

An interactive prototype exploring why students keep using frustrating digital platforms despite deliberately poor UX.

**Core Question**: Why do people continue using a product that frustrates them?
**Central Message**: People do not necessarily continue using a product because they like it. Sometimes, they continue because they cannot avoid it.

The ecosystem includes two parts:

- **CAMPUSLINE**: A university portal for course materials, assignments, grades, attendance, discussions and notifications. It delivers mandatory academic functions while embedding intentional friction.
- **MISSPAY**: A simulated payment app supporting WeChat Pay, Alipay and bank card flows. Tagline: *YOUR MONEY. OUR RULES.* It looks polished but adds unnecessary complexity to simple transactions.

## Engineered Friction

01 — Access & Navigation
Complex math CAPTCHA, no clickable navigation links, manual page name input, repeated confirmation popups.

02 — Interface Instability
Uncomfortable colour & typography; all UI elements shift randomly every 5 seconds.

03 — Notification Overload
Reminders every 15 seconds; app rating prompts every 20 seconds.

04 — Automatic Logout
Auto logout after 60 seconds.

05 — Impossible Assessment
Binary scoring (0 or 100 only); 60-second opening & submission windows; retries reset to question 1.

06 — Anxiety-Inducing Grades
Grades hidden in riddles; slow 8-second animated reveal.

07 — Random Course Identification
Courses displayed only by codes.


# CAMPUSLINE

> 
> 反向产品创新 | 赛道 02

**标语**：一个让你感到烦躁，却不得不继续使用的大学学习平台

交互式原型，探究学生为何持续使用体验糟糕的数字平台。

**核心问题**：人们为什么持续使用一款让自己难受的产品？
**核心主旨**：人们持续使用产品未必是因为喜欢它，有时只是别无选择。

生态包含两个模块：

- **CAMPUSLINE**：高校门户平台，提供课程资料、作业提交、成绩、考勤、讨论、通知等刚需学术服务，同时内置刻意设计的使用摩擦。
- **MISSPAY**：虚拟支付应用，支持微信、支付宝、银行卡模拟支付。标语：*你的钱，我们的规则*。界面精致，但给简单交易增加大量冗余步骤。

## 刻意设计的使用摩擦

01 — 访问与导航
复杂数学验证码、无点击导航栏、需手动输入页面名称、反复确认弹窗。

02 — 界面不稳定
刺眼配色与字体；所有 UI 元素每 5 秒随机移位。

03 — 消息轰炸
每 15 秒推送提醒；每 20 秒弹出评分请求。

04 — 自动登出
60 秒无操作自动下线。

05 — 难以完成的考核
只有 0 分 / 100 分两种结果；开放与提交窗口各仅 60 秒；重答直接回到第一题。

06 — 制造焦虑的成绩展示
成绩以谜题形式呈现；8 秒动画缓慢揭晓。

07 — 课程编码识别
课程仅以编码展示。
